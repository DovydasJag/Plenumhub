About

A small daily word puzzle, made by one person.

Every day there's one pool of letters and three categories, the same for everyone. You make a word for each category and try to leave as few tiles as you can. A new puzzle turns up at midnight, wherever you are.

The word lists

The lists are put together by hand. They stick to words most adults would know, and they take British and American spellings alike: colour or color, grey or gray. Anything arguable is left out, which is why there's no category for things that are blue.

If a word you think should count doesn't, or one that shouldn't does, say so on the contact page.

Par

Par is worked out by a program that tries every combination the lists allow. You can't beat it, but you can match it, and some days there's more than one way to get there.

Paying for it

There's an advert below the puzzle, which covers the hosting. There are no accounts, and your stats stay in your browser. The privacy policy has the details.
